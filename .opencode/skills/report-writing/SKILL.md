# Report Writing

Write from saved evidence rather than chat history. Each finding needs an affected asset, confidence, reproduction evidence, demonstrated impact, severity rationale, and actionable remediation.

Recalculate CVSS from the observed conditions and include the vector. Verify CWE and references. Keep unverified leads out of confirmed findings and executive conclusions. Describe coverage and tool limitations when they affect assurance.

Use concise titles, reproducible steps, redacted evidence, and remediation that addresses the root cause. Separate technical impact from business context and avoid unsupported financial, legal, or compliance claims. The analyst reviews and delivers the report.
